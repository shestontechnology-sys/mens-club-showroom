# Men's Club Atelier - Indian Menswear & Couture Showroom

Where Tradition Meets Modern Style. Premier luxury menswear, ethnic couture, and apparel showroom in Thiruvallur & Chennai.

## Features
- **Curated Haute Couture**: Certified Manyavar kurtas & sherwanis, Nalli silk sarees, Levi's denim, Zara casuals, H&M, and Puma sportswear.
- **Bespoke Tailoring**: Made-to-measure suiting, handcrafted sherwanis, and 2-Hour Express alterations.
- **Atelier AI Fashion Stylist (Chatbot)**:
  - Floating luxury glassmorphic widget with neon lime accents (`#84CC16`).
  - Interactive fashion consultation, collection recommendations with image cards, sizing advice, and showroom directions.
  - Direct integration with Salon Appointment Booking (`#booking-modal`) and WhatsApp concierge.
  - Persistent conversation history across sessions.
